Safety preview: proposals and consent are retained. No deletion is sent until remote target pinning and independent rollback protection are implemented.

How it works

The default activity window is 14 days. Every recent viewer must answer Yes for their latest activity snapshot. Silence or No blocks deletion until that activity leaves the verified window. New activity requires fresh consent and a new full delay of at least 48 hours after eligibility.

Only the original proposal owner may Undo before execution is claimed. An uncertain operation is never automatically resent. Reading this page, the notification bell or Help does not record activity, approve deletion or mark notices read.

Notification preferences, subscriptions and Mark read each require a separate explicit action.
